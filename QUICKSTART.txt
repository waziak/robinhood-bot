📊 WHAT THE BOT DOES

Entry: Scores each stock on 4 signals (SMA trend, RSI, MACD, pullback)
       → Triggers on 3-of-4 signals (medium-risk balance)

Position Sizing: Uses HRP (Hierarchical Risk Parity) to allocate capital
                 → Avoids sector concentration
                 → Weights based on volatility & correlation

Exits: Profit target (+4%), Hard stop (-2%), Trailing stop (-1.5% after +2%)

Risk Gate: Halts new entries if portfolio down >8%

📈 ENTRY SIGNAL BREAKDOWN (all 4 possible)

1. SMA Trend      → SMA20 > SMA50 (5-min candles)
2. RSI Zone       → RSI(14) between 40–58 (momentum confirmation)
3. MACD Cross     → MACD above signal line (bullish)
4. Pullback       → Price within ±2% of SMA20 (support entry)

Example entry log:
  AAPL @ $192.50 | score=3/4 | SMA20(191.80) > SMA50(189.50) | 
                              RSI=42.3 (in zone) | Pullback to SMA20 (-0.21%)

❓ TROUBLESHOOTING

No space for pip install?
  → Use virtual environment: python3 -m venv venv

Bot won't authenticate?
  → Check .env has RH_USERNAME and RH_PASSWORD
  → If MFA enabled, look for "Please enter MFA code" prompt

No entries ever fire?
  → Check entry score logs (all 8 symbols log scores 0–4)
  → Try lowering MIN_ENTRY_SCORE to 1 to force test
  → Verify running during market hours (9:30–16:00 ET, Mon–Fri)

All symbols score 0/4?
  → Market is weak (no uptrends)
  → Adjust RSI_MIN/RSI_MAX in config.py if too strict
